# Data Analytics Business Cases Solution

## 📊 Overview

This repository documents solutions to **real-world Data Analytics business cases** using multiple analytics tools and technologies.

The objective is not simply to learn individual tools, but to develop the ability to take a **business problem, analyze the data, identify meaningful insights, and communicate data-driven recommendations**.

> **Business Problem → Data → Transformation → Analysis → Insight → Decision**

## 🎯 Objective

My goal is to help learners strengthen their Data Analytics skills by solving business cases using different technologies.

Through these projects, I focus on:

- Business problem solving
- Data cleaning and transformation
- Exploratory Data Analysis
- Data manipulation
- Statistical analysis
- KPI development
- Data visualization
- Business intelligence
- Analytical thinking
- Data-driven decision making

## 🧩 Business Case Solving Approach

For each business case, I follow a structured analytical process.

### 1. Understand the Business Problem

- What is the business problem?
- What decision needs to be made?
- Who is the stakeholder?
- What questions need to be answered?

### 2. Explore the Data

- Dataset structure
- Columns and data types
- Missing values
- Duplicate records
- Outliers
- Data quality issues
- Relationships between variables

### 3. Clean & Transform the Data

Depending on the requirement, I use:

- Power Query M
- SQL
- Python/Pandas
- Excel

### 4. Analyze the Data

I use appropriate analytical techniques to answer the business questions, such as:

- Aggregation
- Ranking
- Filtering
- Segmentation
- Trend analysis
- Variance analysis
- Contribution analysis
- Correlation
- Statistical analysis

### 5. Build KPIs & Metrics

Where appropriate, I develop business metrics such as:

- Revenue
- Profit
- Growth %
- Margin %
- Customer count
- Average order value
- Retention rate
- Conversion rate
- Market share
- Product contribution

### 6. Generate Business Insights

The final objective is to answer:

> **What happened?**  
> **Why did it happen?**  
> **What is likely to happen?**  
> **What should the business do?**

## 💼 Example Business Questions

### Sales

- Which products generate the highest revenue?
- Which products have declining sales?
- Which regions contribute most to revenue?
- What are the monthly and yearly growth trends?

### Customers

- Who are the highest-value customers?
- Which customers are becoming inactive?
- What customer segments generate the most revenue?
- Which customers contribute most to the decline in sales?

### Products

- Which products have the highest profit margin?
- Which products are underperforming?
- Which supplier or category performs best?
- What are the highest-priced products by supplier country?

### Finance

- What is driving the change in profit?
- Which business units have the highest expenses?
- What is the variance between budget and actual?
- Which factors contribute most to financial performance?

### HR

- What is the employee turnover rate?
- Which departments have the highest turnover?
- Which employee groups are most likely to leave?
- What factors may be associated with employee attrition?

## 🔄 Same Business Problem — Multiple Technologies

An important part of this practice is learning how the **same business problem can be approached using different technologies**.

For example:

**Business Question:**

> Which products generated the highest sales in each region?

Possible approaches:

- Excel Dynamic Arrays
- Power Query
- SQL
- Python
- DAX

The objective is to understand **the analytical logic**, not simply memorize syntax.
